Who I Am

About me

Rooted in Tunisia, growing in every direction.

I am a 2nd-year preparatory cycle student in Biomedical Engineering at ISTMT, with a deep focus on medical devices, robotics, and healthcare technology.

My academic path began with a Baccalauréat in Experimental Sciences with Honours. I have since built a solid foundation in both engineering principles and clinical environments — including a hands-on internship in radiology and medical imaging at Clinique Zaghouan.

Alongside my studies, I serve as Head of External Relations for Club Biomed Innov, where I lead partnerships, sponsorships, and student events — combining technical ambition with communication and leadership.

I believe in showing up whole — intellectually rigorous, creatively curious, and driven by a genuine desire to contribute to human health through technology.

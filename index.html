About Me

Rooted in Tunisia, growing in every direction.

I am a second-year Biomedical Engineering student in the Preparatory Cycle at ISTMT, driven by a strong interest in medical devices, robotics, and innovative healthcare technologies.

Beyond the classroom, I have gained practical exposure to clinical environments through a hands-on internship in radiology and medical imaging at Clinique Zaghouan, where I developed valuable insights into the intersection of technology and patient care.
